Simulation Assumptions

Educational 24-hour model with one-hour intervals. Solar and wind profiles are illustrative, not measured weather data. Battery starts at 50% state of charge. Charging and discharging efficiencies are each 95%. Battery power is limited to 0.5C. Excess generation is exported and remaining deficits are imported. Grid exchange is assumed unconstrained. Network voltage, frequency and protection are not modelled. This is not a validated grid-design tool.
